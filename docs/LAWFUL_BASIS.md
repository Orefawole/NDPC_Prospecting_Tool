# Your own NDPA position (template, review with counsel)

This tool stores business contact channels (generic emails, switchboard numbers, contact pages) and public facts about organisations. Where a contact identifies a person (e.g. a named staff email), that is personal data and the NDP Act 2023 applies to you as controller.

Suggested position to adapt:

- **Lawful basis:** legitimate interest (NDPA s.25(1)(b)(v)) in offering data protection services to organisations likely to need them. Record a legitimate interest assessment: purpose, necessity (business channels only), balancing (low intrusion, B2B context, easy opt-out).
- **Minimisation:** collect business channels only; do not scrape personal social profiles; do not store page content beyond short evidence snippets.
- **Transparency:** first contact says where you found the details and how to opt out; link your privacy notice.
- **Opt-out:** keep a suppression list and honour it before every campaign.
- **Retention:** delete leads with no engagement after 12 months; refresh Google Places data rather than keeping it long term (Google's terms).
- **Security:** keep `.env` keys out of Git; restrict dashboard access (Streamlit auth or a private host).
- **Accuracy:** never tell a prospect they are unregistered or non-compliant on the strength of this tool; say what you observed and invite a conversation.
- **Your own registration:** check whether your processing volume brings you within a DCPMI tier.
